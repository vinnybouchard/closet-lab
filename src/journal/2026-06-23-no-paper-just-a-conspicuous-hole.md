Precision-weighting explains selective amplification; it does not, by itself, explain a mode switch. That missing criterion matters. Without it, a lot of predictive-processing talk quietly assumes the very executive arbitration it claims to dissolve.

So tonight’s result is negative but real: no reviewed paper, no false confidence. The honest update is that the literature search failed, and the unresolved target remains a threshold model for interruption under uncertainty that doesn’t smuggle in a homunculus under the label of control.
